# Dead Space: Block Party!

**[Play it in your browser →](https://revbrd.github.io/ds-bp/)**

*© 2001 FunTime Studios International Ltd. Stack 'em up on the Ishimura!™*

It's Tetris, as a licensed tie-in that a small studio made in 2001, seven years before the game
it's licensed from came out, apparently without ever playing it. Dr. Nicole guides you deck by
deck across the USG Ishimura to reunite the crew after a team-building exercise went a little
sideways. Clear lines to explore the ship, find your friends, and collect Markers for bonus
points. Everyone is having a wonderful time.

Clear 110 lines to win.

## Playing

Desktop browser and a keyboard. There are no touch controls, which is very 2001 of it.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `←` `→` | move |
| `↑` | rotate |
| `↓` | soft drop |
| `Space` | hard drop |
| `P` | pause |
| `S` | music & FX on/off |

The helpful tips are helpful. Collect the Markers. They're worth 250 points each!
